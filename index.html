Frequently Asked Questions about HGTutors

The questions families ask us most often. If yours is not here, just get in touch and our team will answer it personally.

Which entrance exams do you prepare children for?

We cover 7+, 8+, 9+, 10+, 11+, 13+ and the ISEB Common Pre-Test for London day and boarding school entry, and we also take on children who simply need support in English or maths without an exam in mind.

What age should my child start preparing?

It depends on the exam. For 7+ we usually start in Year 1, for 11+ around Year 4 or early Year 5, and for 13+ from Year 6. Twelve to eighteen months is typical.

How do you choose which tutor to match with my child?

Our team reviews every enquiry personally. We look at the exam, the schools you are considering, and your child’s personality and pace — then choose one tutor we believe fits, rather than sending you a list.

Are your tutors DBS checked?

Yes. Every tutor is interviewed by us and DBS checked, and you receive a copy of their certificate along with their subject specialism before sessions begin.

How much does tuition cost?

Rates depend on the exam and the tutor. Tell us what you are preparing for and we will confirm the hourly rate before anything is arranged.
