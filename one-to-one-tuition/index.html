A boutique agency, not a database

Helen Grogan Tuition is a boutique agency. We do not hand you a list of names and leave you to choose — our team reviews every enquiry personally and matches your child to one tutor suited to their needs, their pace and their personality.

Every tutor is individually interviewed and DBS checked by us. Many also teach at Saturday School, so we have seen first-hand how they work with children before we ever match them.
