why families choose us

A boutique agency, not a database

Helen Grogan Tuition is a boutique agency. We do not hand you a list of names and leave you to choose — Helen reviews every enquiry herself and matches your child to one tutor suited to their needs, their pace and their personality.

Matched personally by Helen

Every tutor is individually interviewed and DBS checked by us. Many also teach at Saturday School, so we have seen first-hand how they work with children before we ever match them.

Expert Guidance, Exceptional Results

We work with children aged 6 to 13 preparing for competitive London day and boarding school entry, and with families who simply want steady support in English or maths.
